Build your stacks. Drag a habit block into a row; left to right is the order you do them. The small × on a block takes that habit out of that stack and leaves it alone everywhere else. There is no save button and you do not need one. Every change is written to your database about a second after you make it, half finished stacks included, so you can close this and come back to exactly where you were. Nothing shows in The PY Week in Review unless you tick in the Week in Review on that stack, and it only counts on the days you have given it, so a weekday stack is not marked down at the weekend. A habit may sit in as many stacks as you like.
