My Bucket List v1.1

One list, ten categories, from The Bucket List Journal by Ben Nemtin. Everything bucket-list shaped lives here, so there is never a second copy somewhere else. The cue card that feeds it is Bucket List on the Cue Cards board.

This list gets one check-in a month, on purpose. Bucket list items move over months, not days, so a daily nudge would only turn into noise you stop reading. The check-in takes a couple of minutes: see what moved, give the quiet ones a new step or park them, and pick the one item that is in motion next.
