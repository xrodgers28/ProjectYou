How to read this. Every number is a rank against your own history, not against other people. 50 means you are exactly average for you. 84 means only 16% of your recorded days were better. The headline is the average of the domains that have data, and it says how many that was — so a 40 out of two domains is never mistaken for a 40 out of five.
