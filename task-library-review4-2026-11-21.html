Why 6 Irvine looked different from TreadWell, and the fix

You were right and it was my inconsistency. TreadWell is a project sitting inside Projects, so its parts were drawn one level deeper and got the plain white treatment. 6 Irvine is a list, so its parts were drawn one level up and got the tinted treatment, even though DIY and Habits Dashboard are doing exactly the same job.

Now the rail goes by what a row is, not how deep it sits. A row that holds things inside it is tinted. A row that is the end of the line is plain white. DIY, Garden, P&G, Habits Dashboard and Activities: Boston all look identical now, because they all are the same thing.

What Parked does, and what it does now

In the mock Parked was only a label. It said a project was resting and should stay out of your Today list, but nothing enforced it, because the mock was not wired to anything.

So I did both. Parked is still a label, now spelled "Parked, keep out of Today" so it says what it means. And underneath it there is now a real "Park it, move to Parking Lot" which actually picks the list up and moves it into Parking Lot, with six seconds to undo.
