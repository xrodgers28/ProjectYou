Features

What every tag, shelf and button on this page does.

The three shelves
⚡ Now
Worked in the next day slot: 10am, 1pm or 4pm Eastern. The header shows which slot is next.
🌙 Tonight
The Midnight Run, 4:00am Eastern. New pages, anything touching a daily-use page, anything risky.
💡 Later
Ideas. Each gets a one-page brief overnight, delivered to Scott's Call; only your go turns it into work.
Tags on a row
Now ▾
The shelf tag. One tap opens the list, a second tap moves the card. Ten seconds of Undo.
Feature · area
What kind of item and which part of the site. A dashed tag is my first-pass label, not yet confirmed by you; tap it to change Feature / Maintenance / Idea.
Quick [15]
How it runs and about how long. Tap to cycle Quick, Background, Overnight, Brief.
⏳ waiting · ⚙ building · ❓ has a question · ✓ done
