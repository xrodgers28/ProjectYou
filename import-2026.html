📥 2026 Import

v1.2
Everything read out of your 2026 calendar sheet, sitting in a holding pen until you say it is right. Fix a name, change a date, switch a row off — nothing moves until you press Send. Rows that go through land on Social and Movies and disappear from here.

A word of warning about the sheet: its month headings are wrong. The block labelled “January 2025” has the weekdays of January 2026, and it drifts from there. Every date below was rebuilt from the weekday row instead, which is internally consistent all the way through.

What everything on this page means

  • The tickWhether this row gets sent. Rows Claude is unsure about, and the 2025 ones, start switched off.
  • Confidence dotgreen means the sheet said it plainly. amber means Claude expanded shorthand. red means Claude is guessing — read the note first.
  • From the sheetThe cell exactly as you typed it, so you can always see what Claude changed.
  • Runs of nightsA series watched over several evenings is one row, not six. The badge says how many nights, and the second date is the night you finished.
  • SendWrites the ticked rows to Social or Movies, then clears them from this page. It will not write the same row twice.
  • DropRemoves a row from the holding pen for good. It never reaches your lists.
