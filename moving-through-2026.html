Project YOU · Modes of Transport · v1.6 · Oct 6, 2026

Moving through 2026: five poster concepts

All five are built on your real data from Jan 1 to Oct 6, 2026: Strava, Metro-North office days, Flighty and TripSource flights, Uber and Waymo receipts, Amtrak, ferry, Citi Bike and, new in this version, your OMNY subway and bus taps. Oct 7 to Dec stay blank. Pick your favourites at the bottom.

Riffs onYour "Eficiência energética" bar chart, now in US units
The ideaRank every mode by energy per passenger-mile, with your real miles beside it. The tabs swap what the bar means: three shades for why, when, or where you moved.
What it revealsAlmost all your energy went into the one mode at the bottom of the chart.
Riffs on"Metro-polis: The Universal Commuter"
The ideaEach mode gets its own lane: track, sidewalk, runway, trail, lift, water or highway. Lane length is hours aboard, all to one scale. Pies on the right show the share of each mode spent waiting.
What it revealsMetro-North is the longest road in town, half again as long as flying. The subway, at about 23 hours, is a short road between walking and Amtrak. The Citi Bike lane is four pixels.
Riffs on"How I've Spent My Time"
The ideaOne flowing stream per family of modes, month by month, with the year's big trips marked as moments.
What it revealsCommuting is the steady river; trips are the floods. April, the LA month, is the widest point.
New conceptA split-flap departures board, like Grand Central's old Solari board.
The ideaEvery mode is a departure. Waiting shows as the delay column, brightness shows how sure we are of it.
What it revealsModes with no data yet sit on the board greyed out, so the gaps are honest, not hidden.
New conceptA 24-hour clock face of when you are actually in motion.
The ideaEach hour is a wedge. The wedge grows with the minutes you spent moving at that hour across the whole year.
What it revealsYour day has two rush hours and a quiet Friday.

Sources: Strava (272 activities after removing watch and phone duplicates), train-platform photo GPS plus Swarm (75 office days, the last 7 inferred from the weekday pattern, 69 minutes each way), Flighty, TripIt and TripSource (24 flight legs), Uber export and receipts, Waymo, Amtrak, Steamship Authority and Citi Bike receipts, and OMNY (59 subway and bus taps, Jan 13 to Oct 6; three taps on May 28 that were another rider's are removed). OMNY records the tap in only, so each ride is estimated at 15 minutes, from Swarm check-ins that land about 15 to 16 minutes after a tap. Energy figures: English Wikipedia, "Energy efficiency in transport", after Banister et al. (1997), converted to BTU per passenger-mile. Train liveries drawn from the Metro-North M8 and Amtrak Northeast Regional (ACS-64 with Amfleet coaches). Carbon is an estimate. Monthly Metro-North split is worked out from the days you walked in Manhattan.
