Where this comes from. Your Swarm check-ins, pulled straight from Foursquare and refreshed four times a day. Every check-in keeps its original record, so nothing here is a guess. A country appears on the map above the moment a check-in proves you were there.

Trips  

These are inferred, not imported. TripIt is not connected, so this reads your check-ins instead. A trip is a run of check-ins more than 75km from home that spans at least one night. 75km is chosen so your whole commute counts as home: Manhattan is 49km out, JFK 45km, Newark 66km. A new trip starts when more than three days pass, or when a check-in back inside that home range proves you came home in between.

Where it gets this wrong. Two trips read as one when you flew straight from one to the next without checking in at home, so the Jul 25 to Aug 3, 2026 run shows San Diego, Tijuana and Boston together when it was really two journeys. A trip with no check-ins at all is invisible, and a day trip with no overnight is left out on purpose. Under the historical-only rule a trip appears only once it has ended, so anything still running is not here yet.

Someday  

Why this sits apart. The system tracks only what has happened, and a wish list is the opposite of that. Someday lives here for convenience, keeps the "add [place] to Future Travel" capture, and is excluded from every count on this page. A place moves into Countries only when a real check-in or a completed trip proves you went.

Your someday map. Every dream destination tagged by country, nearest city, and who put you onto it. Tip: say "add [place] to Future Travel" in any Claude chat and it lands right here.
