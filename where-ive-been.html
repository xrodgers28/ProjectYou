Nothing here until Swarm is connected

You log every single visit in Swarm, which makes it the most detailed record you own. The Foursquare check-in API is free and open, so one authorization backfills your whole history and then keeps itself current.

Trips completed trips only

✈️

Nothing here until TripIt is connected

All your trip information goes into TripIt and is saved there historically, so it is the natural spine for this view. The API is closed to new integrations, so the route in is the read-only calendar feed on your account rather than a developer key.

One rule applies here. A trip has a start and an end, so a trip that has not finished is future data. This timeline will show a trip only once its end date has passed.

Someday  

Why this sits apart. The system tracks only what has happened, and a wish list is the opposite of that. Someday lives here for convenience, keeps the "add [place] to Future Travel" capture, and is excluded from every count on this page. A place moves into Countries only when a real check-in or a completed trip proves you went.
