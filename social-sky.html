Why a sky

It handles the thing the rings cannot

  • Time is the whole interaction. A ring chart shows one moment. A sky can be run forward, so you watch friendships light up and fade instead of reading a date column.
  • Nobody is ever deleted. A person you have not seen in two years is a faint outline, still in their constellation. That is a kinder and more truthful model than dropping them off the chart.
  • Groups keep their shape without needing a ring. Constellation lines only join people inside the same group, drawn as a chain, never a hub.
  • It survives a thin record. – stars is a sparse sky, which is fine. The same number of dots in a bubble chart looks broken.

What it would take

To make this real

  • Nothing new in the database. Every value on screen comes from rows you already have, read through the two connection views.
  • Star positions are fixed by a seeded shuffle inside each constellation, so a person keeps their place between visits. New people appear at the edge of their group.
  • The one thing worth adding is who else was there. A party with six named people would draw six stars, not one, and the constellations would start to mean something.
  • A star is only as true as the log behind it. A quiet corner of the sky may be a quiet season or a month you forgot to write anything down.
