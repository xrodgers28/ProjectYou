“Coffee” is decided in exactly one place — the Postgres function is_coffee_checkin(). It counts Foursquare’s Coffee Shop, Café and Donut Shop categories plus venues explicitly named for coffee, and deliberately excludes bakeries, bagel shops, tea rooms and restaurants merely named “…Cafe”. Widen or narrow it there and every number on this page follows.
