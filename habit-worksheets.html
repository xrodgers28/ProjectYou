How this worksheet works — every control, in one place

The two states every habit has

  • ACTIVE / OFF — the pill under the star. Active means the habit is live: it counts toward your time totals, fills its slice of the Time Budget Dial, and is pushed to Daily Habits and Todays Tasks. Off means it sits on the sheet doing nothing.
  • The star — makes an active habit required. Required habits sort to the top and the day is not complete until they are done. Starring a habit switches it on; switching it off un-stars it.

Finishing things — three different jobs

  • Completed today, in Active Habits Summary, is the day-to-day one. It ticks today’s entry in your to-do list and records the minutes it actually took, so it reaches the Habits Tracker. Put the real number in the took ___ min box first; it starts at your planned figure. This is what a daily or weekly habit uses, every time. If the habit is not on today’s list yet there is nothing to tick, and the page says so rather than pretending.
  • Done for this time and All Done, in Adjust Habits, are for the occasional stuff only — habits set to Monthly or Annually. Daily and weekly habits do not get these buttons at all, because a habit you do every day has no business sitting in a Delivered pile.
  • Done for this time = did it, it comes back. The clock starts the moment you press it, off the habit’s own cadence — a monthly habit reads back on the same date next month. In the Delivered section each one carries back in a week / month / year and a date box, so you can overrule that clock whenever the real answer is different. Set the date and it wins; leave it and the cadence decides.
  • All Done = this one is over and never returns. A week-long project you actually delivered goes here, and stays as the record of what you did.
  • Put back in the Delivered section returns anything to the active list right now, and clears its return date.
  • Nothing sitting in Delivered counts toward your time totals, the dial, or Daily Habits.

Cue cards and trackers

  • + connect in the Habit Tracker column opens the picker: every cue card on the site, and the apps you track in. Tick as many as apply. The first real cue card becomes the link the task opens from Todays Tasks.
  • Drag a pill from one habit to another. Pick it up and drop it on another habit’s Habit Tracker cell — it leaves the row it came from and joins the row it lands on. Hold Ctrl or Cmd while dropping to copy it instead of moving it.

Moving things around

  • The ⋯ button in the far-left column of any row: move to the parking lot, or delete this habit.
  • Parking lot — a holding area at the bottom of the sheet for anything you are unsure about. Nothing there counts toward any total. From there, the Move to… menu sends a habit to any section on any of the seven sheets, so a habit can go from Mental Fitness to Social Well-Being without being retyped. Put back returns it to this sheet.
  • Delete asks first, then removes the habit from the worksheet and from Daily Habits. Anything already logged against it in the tracker is kept.
  • The grip handle (⠯) drags a habit up and down within its own sheet to reorder it.
  • The grip on a section header drags the whole section — header and every habit under it — into the order you want. The new order is saved, so the sheet opens the way you left it.
  • + add habit on any section header adds a row straight into that section, with the cursor already in the name box. It is not saved until you give it a real name.

Editing

  • Every text box on the sheet is editable in place — habit name, insight, outcome, identity, section label. Click and type. Enter commits and closes the box; Shift+Enter puts in a line break.
  • Everything saves on its own about a second after you stop typing. The pill in the bottom right corner tells you where it is up to: saving, saved, or an error worth reading.

Reading the two panels at the top

  • Total Daily Habits Assigned — this sheet only, spread across the year, so a weekly 70-minute habit reads as 10 minutes a day.
  • Total Habit Time, All Seven Sheets — the same figure for everything you have switched on anywhere. The dial beside it gives each Compass section its own slice; the slice grows with the number of active habits in it and carries that number. Faint slices are empty sections.

Habits already running that are not on a worksheet

  • Adopt turns one into a worksheet row you can switch on and off like the rest. Dismiss drops it off the list and leaves it running exactly as it is. Dismissed items can be shown again from the same panel.
