Two things decide most of it
Getting what you want out of life largely boils down to two things.
01 The story you tell yourself
What story do you tell yourself about what has happened to you, what you're capable of, and what you hope to achieve?
02 Where you direct your attention
Are you taking control and directing your attention, or merely letting inertia pull you along?
Master your internal monologue. Master your daily attention.
James Clear, author of Atomic Habits
