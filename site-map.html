How this page stays honest

Refreshing it

The page holds no facts of its own.Everything above is drawn from site-map-data.json, rebuilt by the crawler at site-map-crawl.py. To refresh the map, re-run the crawler and publish the new data file. The page itself does not need republishing, and its "last checked" stamp comes from the data, never from your clock.
