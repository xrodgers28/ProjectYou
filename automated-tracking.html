Connected now

● Live Real sources sending data into the system today.
Verified row-by-row against the database. Six outside sources write data: Strava (activities → todos + qs_log, every 2 hours), Drafts (dictated notes → inbox, then routed to to-dos, travel, movies, quotes or connections), Apple Health (→ health_metrics, pushed hourly from the phone), the Apple Watch behind it, which is the named source on most of those readings, Swarm (check-ins → checkins, backfilled to April 2009) and, newest of all, Waking Up, whose meditation sessions began writing mindful minutes on Aug 15 2026. Apple ships no cloud API for health data, so the Apple Health feed only exists because Health Auto Export pushes it off the phone. It is listed here as a source in its own right, so the dependency is never forgotten. The blue connectors, Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Supabase, 1Password and the Claude desktop app, are authenticated at the account level and power the wider operating system, but none of them writes its own tracking feed. What each source has actually contributed is counted at the bottom of this page.
