What the five buttons mean

  • Do this — go and build it, run it, ship it. An instruction, not an answer. The row stays open until the work is finished.
  • Tell me more — you want to say yes but need the cost, the risk or the reasoning first. The row stays open and the next move is mine.
  • I did it — you handled it yourself. The row settles.
  • I read this — just a note that you saw it. Nothing is owed. The row settles.
  • Kill this task — dead, and it never comes back. The row settles.
