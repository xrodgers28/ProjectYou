03 · Design history

How the design has moved

Every version stays published at its own link. Newest first.

v0.30 · Nov 21, 2026 (this page)
Scott asked for the plus tile and a menu on each list. The plus tile was already there and working. The menu was not: it only showed on the header of the list you already had open, so nine of the ten lists had nothing to tap, even though the help line promised it. The same menu now sits on every list row in the rail, faint until you point at it, and always visible on iPhone. He also confirmed keeping all three of the lists I suggested: Health and appointments, Waiting On, Someday and ideas.
