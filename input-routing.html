How every note you dictate gets tagged and where it lands. Tap a row to see what gets written. Scott’s Call is the page where you approve; this page is the map behind it.

How a note travels
Every note carries the same five things
🍿 PopcornYour exact words, untouched, always.
A plain-English titleOne clean line in your meaning, nothing invented.
The interpreted noteShort dictation: one sentence. Long: two to four simple, clear sentences.
A tag and a projectThe one-word kind below, plus 🎡 Project YOU, 🥾 TreadWell or 💼 Amazon. Together they pick the buttons, the guess and the two runners-up on the row.
Estimate + confidence dotMinutes guessed on arrival. Green is sure, red means Claude wants your eyes.
