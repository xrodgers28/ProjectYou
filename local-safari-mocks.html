What this is
One new tile for the Week in Review. It shows only places you had never checked into before, pulled from your Swarm check-ins. Regular spots never appear. Offices and stores are hidden as errands. Every mock below uses the real week of Sep 14, 2026: Tartinery Café (Tue), Black Press Coffee (Thu) and Taco Tequila Taqueria in Norwalk (Sat). EuroOptica was hidden as an errand. Tap the pill or pin in any mock to switch which place is the hero. The very quiet "NYC map" prompt sits at the bottom right of each tile, only on New York places. Type a comment under any mock, tap Pick this one on your favourite, answer the two questions at the foot, then Copy my answers and paste them into the chat.
OPTION A

Postcard

Your own photo, full bleed, one place at a time. Feels like a postcard you sent yourself. When a place has no photo it falls back to a small map. Best if the point is the memory, not the geography.
OPTION B

Map of the week

A real map (free OpenStreetMap, no key, no cost) with a green pin on each new place. Tap a pin for the name, day and your photo if you took one. Best if the point is seeing how far you roamed.
OPTION C

Passport stamps

Each new place is a stamp in a passport. Tap a stamp to open it. Gamified and collectable, and it grows into a book over the year. Best if the point is the habit and the count.
OPTION D

The ledger

One big number, the list underneath, and an eight week strip showing how each past week compared. Reads like the rest of the Week in Review. Best if you want it to sit quietly in the page and match the other numbers.
OPTION E

Range rings

Home in the middle, rings at 5, 20 and 50 miles, each new place plotted by how far from Old Greenwich it is. Shows reach, not streets. Best if "go further" is the habit you want to reinforce.
