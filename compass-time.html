Compass Time

This decides two things for every habit: which Clarity Compass point it belongs to, and whether its minutes should count toward your daily and weekly habit-time totals. It does not change your habits, your day, or your schedule, only how those totals get built. Every habit already carries a Compass point from the Habit Worksheets; this page is where you confirm each one or correct it, and decide what counts.

What this feeds. Once you've been through this list, I'll wire the Time Bandit Wheel, the Clarity Compass wheel, the Week in Review and the Habits Tracker totals to read from these same choices, so all four agree with each other and with this page.
