🎬 Movies

v2.4
Two halves. Coming up is what you are part way through and what is queued, each one tagged with where it streams so you are not hunting for it at 9pm. Seen is your history, newest first, dated and rated out of five. Every title can carry a Source, so you remember who put you onto it. Say "add [title] to my Movies board" in any Claude chat and it lands here.

📥 The 2026 calendar sheet is waiting to be checked over on the Import page. Nothing from it reaches this board until you send it.

What everything on this page means

  • SourceWho or what put the title on your list. A person, a publication, anything. Shown as the green tag on the card.
  • StarsYour rating out of five, on titles you have seen. Click a star to set it, click the same star again to clear it.
  • Seen dateStamped automatically the moment you mark something seen, using the ProjectYou day that runs 2am to 2am Eastern. Click the date to change it.
  • Is this the one?An amber card is a title Claude had to interpret from your shorthand. Confirm it or type the right name, and the flag clears.
  • EXAMPLEA dashed, faded card is a placeholder Claude put in so the Seen lane was not blank. None of them are your real history. Clear examples in the Seen header removes them all at once.
  • Where it streamsThe blue 📺 tag on a queued title says which service has it in the US. Amber means it will cost money, or Claude could not confirm a home for it — hover for the detail and the date it was checked.
  • PosterPulled from Wikipedia the first time a card is drawn, then saved on the record so it loads instantly after that.
