Reading it

Four things carry meaning

  • Distance from you is the ring you put them in. Inside the ring band, a recent visit pulls them toward the inner edge, so the band itself has a slope.
  • Size is how many times they appear in the record. The biggest circle on the map is –, at – logged, last on –.
  • Brightness is recency. Full colour means the last three months, a ghost outline means over a year.
  • A dashed edge means nobody has said how close this person is. The map guessed from their group and is telling you so.

What it is not

The honest limits

  • Age is recorded for – of – entries, so the age fan parks the rest in a tray under the horizon rather than pretending.
  • – entries are occasions, not people. Christmas is not a person. They are off by default.
  • – entries have no group set and draw grey. Fixing one is a single edit on the Social log.
  • Everything here comes from what got logged. A quiet ring may mean a quiet friendship or a month you forgot to write anything down.
