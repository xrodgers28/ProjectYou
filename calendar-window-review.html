Why it stops at Wednesday

Not the date window. That is set to fourteen days and it is working. Something else is cutting it short.

There is a cap of 50 events sitting in the shortcut

50the cap

The permission box on your phone told us the number: it asked to send exactly 50 calendar events. Your diary is busy enough that fifty events only reaches Wednesday lunchtime. The shortcut has a limit switch set to fifty, and it is taking the first fifty and stopping.

Raising it is one number, and it is safe up to a point. Because your phone still repeats itself, the message it builds grows fast: fifty events made 1,275 lines, a hundred and twenty would make about 7,000, and two hundred would make twenty thousand and very likely fail on the phone before it ever reaches me. So a full week is a safe change today. The full fortnight needs the repeating fixed first, which is a real edit to the shortcut and the thing you did not want to spend your evening on.
