    How this page works

    Every note you dictate into Drafts lands here and waits. Nothing files itself. The routing table shows where each kind of note goes.

    A waiting note
    For you · For Claude
    Two groups: things for your day and your lists, and things Claude builds or answers. In each, the shaky guesses sit on top and the sure ones can be approved in one tap.
    ● red · amber · green
    How sure I am about the home I picked. Red means read it properly.
    → home ▾ and the two grey tags
    The filled tag is my guess. The two dashed ones are my runners-up: tap one and it becomes the pick, no menu. The ▾ opens the full list.
    2 places
    An amber pill next to the destination means one tap writes in two places: a film goes to Movies and the habit tracker, a person to Connections and the tracker, a habit to the worksheet and the tracker. Hover it to see both. Undo removes both.
    The pinwheel · 🥾 · 💼
    Which project the note belongs to: Project YOU (the pinwheel spins on rollover; on a waiting note a tap sends it straight to Project You Ideas, as on the Staging Area), TreadWell, Amazon. No icon means personal.
    The headline
    Tap the words to fix a garbled voice note. Under it, the note interpreted in one to four plain sentences. Popcorn keeps your exact words.
    The binoculars
    Look before you file: opens the destination page in a window on top. On a Delivered item it opens the page I actually built, when there is one.
    Approve · Log it · Dismiss
    The button changes with the home. Approve files a to-do or a build item. Log it records a thing that happened and never makes a to-do: a film goes to Movies and a 🎬 square on the tracker, a person to Connections and a Social Fitness square, a habit ticks the worksheet, a meal goes to the Meal log, an appointment to the Calendar and on to Google. Dismiss moves it to a recoverable bin.
    🌙 · ×
    Top right of every row. The moon sends it straight to the Midnight Run (Claude's List, Tonight). The × kills it, with Undo.
    more ▾
    ▶ Do it now (approves it to Claude's List, Now, and asks the runner to start), 🌙 send it to tonight's Midnight Run, Park it, straight to Project You Ideas, Make it a rule, Dismiss.
    🍿 Popcorn · 💬 comment · est · act
    Popcorn shows your dictation word for word, even when one dictation became several rows. Comment rides along with your tap and I remember it. Est is my guess at minutes, act is yours after.
    The green strip after a tap
    Says what was filed and where, with Undo and a correction box. Ten seconds, then it folds into the Archive.
    📷 Add a photo
    A screenshot, a menu, a page of a book. It lands here as a waiting note with the picture; I read it at the next slot and write the note under it.
    ▶ Run now
    Starts the Now shelf of Claude's List. If a chat with me is open I begin within a minute; otherwise the runner takes it at the top of the hour, 10am to 6pm EST. Approved builds land back here under Delivered by Claude. The 4am Midnight Run takes the Tonight shelf on its own.
    ⟳ Pull from Drafts now
    Opens Drafts and runs your Sweep to ProjectYou action. Several tasks in one note get split into separate rows. Otherwise the sweep runs every two hours on its own.
    Delivered by Claude
    Got It
    You have seen it. Files it into the Archive.
    Needs a fix
    Say what is wrong; it becomes a Quick item on the Now shelf of Claude's List.
    Do this · Tell me more · I did it · I read this · Kill this task
    The five buttons, on briefs and questions. Do this and Tell me more keep the row open because work is still owed.
    The rest
    Add to the rules?
    Yes folds the rule into your working rules at the next RAP. No drops it.
    Only you can do this
    Your own items. I did it files them; the moon or Give it to Claude hands one over.
    🅿️ Parking Lot
    The one and only. Undecided things wait here until you move them.
