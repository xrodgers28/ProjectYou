Tracker Animation Ideas

v1.0

Two animation ideas for each of the nine trackers, in the same order as the Daily Habit Tracker page. Press Play under each idea to watch it on that tracker. For every idea, tap Love it, Maybe or Not for me, and type a note if you want something changed. Nothing here touches your real habits: it is all sample data.

Each tracker starts mid-day. The "when you tap" ideas finish one habit. The "when the day is done" ideas finish the last three, then play the big moment.
