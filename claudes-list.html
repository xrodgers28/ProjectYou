Features

What every tag, shelf and button on this page does.

The three shelves
⚡ Now
Worked in the next day slot: 10am, 1pm or 4pm Eastern. The header shows which slot is next.
🌙 Tonight
The Midnight Run, 4:00am Eastern. New pages, anything touching a daily-use page, anything risky.
💡 Later
Ideas. Each gets a one-page brief overnight, delivered to Scott's Call; only your go turns it into work. Parked things are different: they are undecided, so they wait on you, on Scott's Call, not here.
Tags on a row
Now ▾
The shelf tag. One tap opens the list, a second tap moves the card. Ten seconds of Undo.
Habit Modules
The area tag. A plain area means a Feature; Maintenance and Idea say so. Dashed means my first-pass label, not yet confirmed by you; tap it to change.
Quick [15]
How it runs and about how long. Tap to cycle Quick, Background, Overnight, Brief.
