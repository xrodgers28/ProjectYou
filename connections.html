Social Interactions —
Ranked by how far past your own cadence each person is. Last seen is computed from the log, never typed, so it cannot go stale the way a hand-kept column does. The target comes from the circle you put someone in.
The year grid turned ninety degrees. Rows are people, columns are months, a dot is a meeting. Drift reads as a run of empty cells. The window rolls rather than following the calendar, because on Jan 1 a calendar year would wipe the evidence.
Your connection log: every coffee, lunch, dinner and get-together, growing over time. The occasions run in the order a day does; who someone is — family, industry — is a label rather than an occasion. Both match the Import page. Say "add lunch with [name] to my Connections board" in any Claude chat.
