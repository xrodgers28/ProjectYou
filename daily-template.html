Daily Habits

v2.8

Your daily habits, in two zones. Live is what runs today; Staging holds habits that are switched off for now. Drag a row between zones whenever you like. Whatever is in Live becomes your habits automatically at 4:00am each night, so there is nothing to lock in and nothing to remember. Use Apply now only if you want today's list changed before then: it runs exactly the same rules the 4:00am job runs, nothing separate.

This page owns the order, the minutes and what is live. The Habit Worksheets own the habit's name, its section, its purpose and whether it exists at all. If you change one of those here, this page will ask whether to change the Worksheet to match.

Two things this page now handles on its own. A Weekly or Monthly habit only shows up on the day it is actually due, and drops off once you have done it for that week or month, instead of nagging you every day. And four quick taps, Coffee, Water, Today’s Plan and Social, sit on an Always on list: they stay on your day for good and are never cleared, even though they are deliberately not habits in this list.
