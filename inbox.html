Staging Area

v2.5

Everything you capture lands here first. Read it, fix the label, then send it to Today's Todos, the Task Library, or its own list. Tap Import to paste a whole chunk and have it split into tasks. Tapping a tag only changes where it will go. Press the green button at the bottom of a section to file everything in it. Nothing files itself while the switch says Ask first.

Layout Notes from Filing

What keeps this safe

  • Undo lasts 7 days, not one session
  • Anything due in the next 3 days always asks you first
  • Garbled dictation never files itself, and your words stay visible
  • The track record counts only items you opened or confirmed
  • One tap puts it back to Ask first, any time
  • Duplicates are checked across every source, not one at a time
