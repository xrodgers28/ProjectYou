Features

Every label and control on this page, and exactly what it does to your capture.

The confidence dot

  • redLow confidence. Claude is guessing. Read this one properly before you approve it (sorts to the very top)
  • orangeMedium confidence. Probably right, worth a glance
  • greenHigh confidence. Unmistakable. These group under Looks clear and can be cleared in one tap

Row buttons

  • ApproveFiles the capture to the destination shown in the dropdown and takes it off this page. You get about six seconds of Undo
  • Park itOne tap to 🅿️ Parking Lot — the someday-maybe shelf. Use it when the idea is good but not now, instead of forcing a destination
  • DismissThe grey ×. A soft delete: the capture moves to a recoverable Dismissed bin, not the trash. Nothing is destroyed and the original Drafts note stays archived
  • ✓ Approve allClears the whole Looks clear group at once, each to its own guessed destination

Row links

  • 🍿 PopcornShows the raw dictation exactly as you spoke it, before Claude cleaned it up. Use it when the headline reads wrong (popcorn = a loose idea that just popped)
  • 💬 commentTell Claude what is off or where it really belongs. Saves when you click away, and trains the next guess. The 🎤 dictates it instead of typing
  • pre-pickedClaude fills in one of the two dropdowns for you. Personal life pre-selects the Today's To-Do list; work on this site pre-selects Send to All ToDos with a category and an owner underneath it. The one it chose is the solid one, the other is dimmed. Approve follows whichever is picked
  • dropdownChange the destination. Your board sections are read live, so the list is never stale. The Learning modules at the bottom of the list are not to-do sections — see below
  • Send to
    All ToDos    Routes the capture to the All ToDos project board instead of your daily to-do list, into whichever category you pick

Learning modules

  • 🎬
    💬
    ✈️
    👋    Approving to Movie List, Clever Phrase, Future Travel or Friends Visit does not put a to-do on your board. The capture is written into that module's own database and disappears from your to-do list entirely — a movie lands in Movies, a phrase in Clever Phrases, a place in Future Travel, a person in Social. The confirmation strip links straight to the module so you can check it landed, and Undo removes it again
  • noteYour 💬 comment rides along as that record's note: the meaning of a phrase, why you want to go somewhere, what you want to do with that person

The date picker

  • 📅A small calendar appears only on the two destinations that record a real date: 🎬 Movie List asks watch on, 👋 Friends Visit asks meet on. Optional. Leave it empty and the record simply carries no date
  • carriesThe date you pick travels with the capture: into watch_on on the film, or happened_on on the person. Change the destination and the calendar appears or disappears to match

Time boxes

  • estEstimate, in minutes. Claude pre-fills its best guess. Correct it here and the number rides along to your board
  • actActual, in minutes. Fill it in after the task is done. Starts from the estimate so you only change it if you were wrong
  • ±Appears once both are filled: how far off the estimate was. Red means it took longer than you thought, green means shorter
Why both numbers matter: the estimate alone tells you nothing. The gap between est and act is the whole point — after a few dozen tasks the Estimate accuracy line at the bottom of this page tells you your personal fudge factor, so you can stop guessing at how long your day holds.
On “done” versus “×”: there is no done button on this page, because nothing here has been started yet — a capture is an idea waiting for a home. You Approve it onto a board, and you finish it there. The × here means Dismiss: it was never worth doing. On Todays Tasks the two similar glyphs are ✕ skip this habit today and × delete the task outright.
